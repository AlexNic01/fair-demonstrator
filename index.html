FAIR case record · TU Berlin · demonstrator

One fixed-bed case, from experiment to CFD, in one HDF5 file

This page demonstrates the FAIR workflow: an experiment and the automated pipeline that rebuilds it (packed bed, mesh, particle-resolved CFD) are stored in a single, self-describing HDF5 case record with units, provenance and checksums, and shown in a browser viewer.

This is a demonstrator: measurement and simulation do not belong together
Measurement: particle Aa real fixed-bed experiment on the TREx rig (flows, pressures, temperatures)
Simulation: particle Bbed, mesh and CFD computed for a different particle, a generic hollow cylinder

The two were combined on purpose to show the workflow and the interface. The values are not physically consistent and must not be used for validation. Feedback on how the record and viewers are organised and how understandable they are is very welcome, not on the numbers.

Explore the HDF5 file in myHDF5 → Browse every group, attribute and dataset of the case record in the free online viewer (H5Web, ESRF; hosted by the HDF Group). ~79 MB, opens in the browser. Open the HTML viewer → Interactive report built from the same file: rig, operating points, packed bed in 3D, mesh, CFD results, experiment vs CFD, provenance. ~9 MB, best on a laptop.
Experiment→Experiment HDF5→Automated pipeline→Case record HDF5→HDF5 / HTML viewer→You
